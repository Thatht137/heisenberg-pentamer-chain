Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, "Local-spin projection and momentum-selective spectral weight in a Heisenberg pentamer chain," for publication as a Regular Article in Physical Review B.

The manuscript examines how local composite spins determine the visibility of collective magnetic excitations in a spin-1/2 chain with a five-site unit cell. Changing the sign of the outer intra-cell exchange changes the isolated-cell ground multiplet from spin 1/2 to spin 3/2 in the range studied. The resulting redistribution of the projected local moments reverses the relative intensity of antiferromagnetic responses at physical momenta that correspond to the same effective-chain momentum.

The central quantitative result is an absolute low-energy spectral-weight prediction for two weakly coupled pentamers, without fitting an intensity amplitude. Exact diagonalization tests both the projected singlet-triplet gap and the momentum-resolved weight. At six points with inter-cell exchange no larger than one percent of the local excitation gap, the relative L2 error of the weight vector is below 0.27%, and the gap error is below 0.56%. A separate reflection-parity analysis identifies why the lowest excited local doublet can be invisible to the staggered probe.

Complete L=40 spectra and finite-size static correlations complement this controlled weak-coupling analysis. These calculations distinguish momentum-dependent visibility from changes in the underlying collective momentum. The manuscript explicitly separates the strong-coupling spectral comparison from the domain of the quantitative projection test; it does not claim a new thermodynamic phase or a complete phase diagram.

We believe this model-specific connection between local multiplets, absolute spectral weight, and folded magnetic responses will interest readers working on low-dimensional quantum magnetism and dynamical spin correlations. The manuscript presents the numerical controls and the limitations of the finite-size and finite-time results.

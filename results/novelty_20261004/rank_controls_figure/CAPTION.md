# Mechanism and periodic-chain controls

(a) Simple local form-factor roots at 16 sampled common effective momenta Q/pi=0.55,...,1. Quartet physical momenta are q=(Q+4pi)/5; doublet momenta are q=(Q+2pi)/5. Connected points guide the eye, not an interpolation used in calculations. All local gaps remain positive at the sampled roots. This is a local root scan, not a many-body phase diagram.

(b) Exact coefficient r_J=J^2/2-2s(s+1)/3 relating the composite vector X to the spin difference D between neighboring bond-spin sectors J and J-1. Values are algebraic coefficients, not spectral intensities. Ground-state accessibility and interlink interference must still be checked.

(c) Minimum leading inelastic coefficient Cmin at the exact quartet q=pi root on even periodic effective chains. Accepted DMRG points pass the original energy, variance, weight, spin and translation checks. Crosses explicitly denote unaccepted calculations and are not converged evidence. No thermodynamic fit is made.

(d) Effective periodic chains at exactly the same two local roots and Q=2pi/3 as the microscopic three-cell comparison. Quartet optimization is performed separately for each size; it is not the same kappa at all N. The doublet dashed line denotes the first-order operator-cancellation result, not a microscopic zero at finite beta. Doublet dots are ED checks. N=3 is an odd frustrated ring; N=6 and N=12 are even. The N=12 calculation is a twelve-site effective spin-3/2 chain, not microscopic L=60 diagonalization.

Panels (c,d) retain the microscopic normalization 1/sqrt(5N). Their different q and alpha imply different coefficient scales, so they must not be interpreted as a shared size series. No smoothing, reflection averaging, or independent normalization was applied. PDF and SVG retain vector text; PNG is exported at 300 dpi.
